# MamePilot — What the App Can Do

MamePilot is a single, all-in-one dashboard for running an online business. It brings together your customers, orders, products, money, staff, marketing, and customer messages in one place — so you don't have to juggle spreadsheets and separate tools.

---

## 1. Signing In and Who Can See What

- **Secure login** — you sign in with your username and password, and the app remembers you.
- **Three kinds of users** — Admin (runs everything), Developer (technical support), and Employee (day-to-day work).
- **Flexible access control** — every user only sees and does what their role allows. For example, one person may be able to create orders but not delete them, while another can manage finances.
- **Custom roles** — the owner can create new roles with exactly the access they want.
- **Smart landing page** — after logging in, everyone is taken straight to the module they work with most.

## 2. The Dashboard (Your Business at a Glance)

When you open the app you see a ready-made summary of your business:

- Total sales, total purchases, other expenses, and total profit.
- How many orders are at each stage: on hold, being processed, with the courier, picked up, delivered, exchanged, returned, or cancelled.
- Which payments are received, partial, outstanding, overpaid, or refunded.
- A chart of your monthly income, expenses, and profit.
- Your biggest expense categories, best-selling products, and top customers.

**For employees**, the dashboard shows their own workload — orders created, orders pending today, wallet balance, and how they compare with teammates.

The owner can choose which numbers and charts appear on the dashboard, so each team is shown what matters most.

## 3. Sales — Orders

- **Creating an order** — pick the customer, add the items, adjust discount/shipping, and the app calculates the total by itself. You can even type only the customer's name and phone and the app fills the rest in.
- **Adding a little extra** — attach a note, link the order to a Facebook or WhatsApp ad so you know where it came from, and choose which of your brand pages it belongs to.
- **Following the order** — every order moves through clear stages: Created → On Hold → Processing → Courier assigned → Picked → Completed. You can always see what stage it's at and what happened and when.
- **Working the stages** — with one click you can release a held order for processing, hand it to the courier, mark it picked up, or complete it as delivered or returned.
- **Handing orders to a courier** — send a single order or many at once to Steadfast, CarryBee, Paperfly, or Pathao (see Couriers section).
- **Partially delivered orders** — if a customer received only part of the order, record exactly which items came back; the app updates stock, shipping, and payments automatically.
- **Returns and exchanges** — return some or all items, or exchange them for other products. Refunds and extra payments are handled properly, and the exchange is followed through its own courier journey.
- **Duplicate an order** — copy an existing order to reuse it (great for repeat customers).
- **Getting paid** — record partial or full payment against an order. Money goes into the right account and the right payment method.
- **Print the order sheet** — a clean, printable copy ready for packing and delivery.

## 4. Purchases — Bills

- **Creating a bill** — add items you bought from a supplier (vendor), set discount and shipping, and record the total — just like the order form.
- **Following the bill** — bills move through stages: On Hold → Processing → Received, and can be marked Returned or Cancelled.
- **Recording payments** — record how much you've paid the supplier, with the account and payment method used.
- **Returning goods to a supplier** — process a partial return and get a refund recorded properly.
- **Print the bill** — a clean printable copy with your branding.
- **Vendor history** — open any vendor to see all their bills and how much you still owe them.

## 5. Products and Stock

- **Product catalog** — add, edit, and organize every product with a photo, name, code, category, unit, selling price, buying price, and stock count.
- **Bulk pricing rules** — set automatic price breaks (for example, buy 5+ and the price changes) per product.
- **Units of measure** — choose how items are counted (pieces, kg, dozens, and more).
- **Live inventory tracking** — for businesses that sell by counts that change (like poultry or livestock), "batches" let you track the number of animals and their average age in a group:
  - Record events like purchases, deaths, sales, and expenses against a batch, and the count updates itself.
  - See a complete history of every event, who recorded it, and how the count changed.
- **Stock that stays in sync** — every order, bill, return, exchange, or correction updates your stock automatically, so you never have to count by hand.

## 6. Customers and Vendors

- **Customer list** — every customer with their contact details, how many orders they've placed, and any money they owe.
- **Vendor list** — every supplier with their purchase history and what you owe them.
- **Smart form filling** — paste a customer or vendor's details as a simple text (name, phone, address) and the app fills the form in for you.
- **Fraud check on customers** — check a customer's phone number against courier delivery history to see if they are a risky customer (how many parcels were delivered vs. cancelled). You can also turn on an automatic check every time a new order is created.

## 7. Money and Banking

- **Accounts** — keep bank and cash accounts, each with its current balance.
- **Transactions** — record every income, expense, and money transfer with a category, description, payment method, and a photo attachment if needed. Transactions can be linked to a specific order, bill, customer, or vendor.
- **Double-check on big spending** — you can set transactions to require a manager's approval before they count.
- **Transfers** — move money between accounts in a couple of clicks.
- **Scheduled transactions** — set up bills you pay regularly (rent, salaries, subscriptions) to be recorded automatically daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly.
- **Employee wallets** — employees earn commission per completed order into their own wallet. The owner can add bonuses or deductions and pay out the balance through the bank.

## 8. Reports

A set of ready-made reports helps you understand how your business is really doing. All can be filtered by date range and downloaded:

- **Expense summary** — where your money goes each month.
- **Income summary** — how much you earn, average sale size, and your best customers.
- **Income vs. expense** — a monthly comparison showing your best and worst months.
- **Profit & loss** — a full business result: sales, cost of goods, expenses, and real net profit (it can even combine multiple brand pages).
- **Order report** — every order with its stage, customer, helper, cost, and profit.
- **Product sales** — how many units and how much revenue each product brought in.
- **Customer sales** — how many orders and how much money each customer represents.
- **Team performance** — for each employee: how many orders they made, completion and payment rates, average order size, and activity history.

## 9. People and Payroll

- **Staff directory** — a full profile for every employee: photo, contact details, documents (NID, CV), and their pay arrangement (fixed salary or commission).
- **Team overview** — a dashboard to see how the team is performing.
- **Payroll** — calculate each employee's pay for the month automatically from the orders they handled (plus fixed salary, bonuses, or deductions). Approved payments are paid out and recorded in their wallet and the accounts.
- **Employee wallet** — each employee can see their own earnings, bonuses, deductions, and payout history.

## 10. Advertising and Growth

- **Ads dashboard** — connect your Facebook/Meta advertising account and see how it performs against reality:
  - How much you spend vs. how many impressions, clicks, and purchases you get.
  - Which campaigns actually earn delivered revenue (not just bookings that later get cancelled or returned).
  - Cost per sale, delivery rate, and return rate — the numbers that really matter.
  - Line-up of recent orders tied to each ad and campaign.
- **Meta Ads** — safely connect MamePilot to your Meta advertising account, with automatic syncing of campaign performance. Ad spending in foreign currency is converted to BDT automatically.
- **Grow Your Business** — the app uses AI to study your best-selling products and quietly recommends what to sell more of and what to improve, with clear priorities.

## 11. Chatting with Customers (and AI Help)

- **WhatsApp and Messenger inboxes** — connect your WhatsApp Business and Facebook Messenger so every customer conversation lives inside MamePilot. Reply from one place, with customer details beside the chat.
- **Automatic conversations** — the app can greet new customers, show starter questions ("ice breakers"), and hand off to a human teammate when needed.
- **Smart lead tracking** — when a customer messages you, the app reads the conversation and builds a profile automatically: their name, what they're interested in, how likely they are to buy, any objections, and the best next step.
- **Ordering via voice call (auto-calling)** — after an order is created, the app can automatically call the customer to confirm it with a recorded voice survey (taps or voice replies), then record the answer — confirmed, cancelled, or on hold — right on the order.
- **Mame AI assistant** — a chat assistant inside the app that can answer questions and, where allowed, carry out tasks using your business data.

## 12. Couriers and Delivery

- **Connect all major couriers** — set up Steadfast, CarryBee, Paperfly, and Pathao once, then hand over orders with one click.
- **Tracking everywhere** — consignment numbers and tracking links are saved on each order.
- **Automatic updates** — the app stays in touch with the courier so order statuses update on their own (for example, when the courier reports an order as delivered, MamePilot marks it delivered).
- **Automatic shipping costs** — when the courier sends the final shipping bill, the expense is recorded automatically under the right category and account. Optionally, orders can be auto-marked "paid" after delivery.
- **One place for courier settings** — credentials, default accounts, and expense categories for each courier.

## 13. Fraud Protection

- **Check a phone number before shipping** — the app looks up the customer's past delivery history across couriers (delivered vs. cancelled parcels, success rate) so you can spot risky orders before you send them.
- **History and reports** — see past check results and reports for each number.
- **Optionally automatic** — check every new order automatically.

## 14. Mistakes, Accidents, and Data Safety

- **Recycle bin** — accidentally deleted a customer, order, product, or anything else? It goes to a recycle bin where you can bring it back, or remove it permanently.
- **Undo button for orders** — made a mistake on an order, like marking it completed by accident? The app can undo the step and shows you exactly what will change before you confirm (money, stock, wallet, and courier notes).
- **Activity log** — the app quietly keeps a record of who did what, so questions can always be answered.
- **Backups** — simple commands (for the technical team) to make and restore backups of all your data.

## 15. Notifications

- **In-app notification bell** — see unread messages and announcements right inside MamePilot.
- **Actionable alerts** — some notifications need a decision. For example, a teammate may request approval for a large payment — you can approve or decline it directly from the notification.
- **Announcements from the product team** — important updates from MamePilot can be broadcast to you, with a record of who read and acted on them.

## 16. Settings (Your Preferences)

Everything can be set up to match your business:

- **Your brand** — company name, logo, phone, email, address; different brands/pages for different stores, all shown on printed documents; white-label mode (removing MamePilot's name) and a custom theme color.
- **Invoice and order numbers** — choose the prefix and where numbering starts.
- **Defaults** — preset the account, payment method, and categories most used, how many records show on a page, and limits on transaction amounts.
- **Categories and payment methods** — organize income, expense, and product categories and set the payment methods you accept.
- **Payroll rules** — what an order is worth in commission and which order stages count.
- **Permissions, dashboards, couriers** — all the setup touched on above lives here in plain English.

## 17. Plans and Subscriptions

- **Plans control features** — MamePilot plans turn features on or off. If a feature isn't included in your plan, it's simply locked and explained.
- **See your plan** — the admin can see the current plan, renewal date, and when it was last checked — all in one place.
- **Payments** — optional add-on services can be billed through the app, and payments are tracked as approved or pending.
- **If a plan lapses** — the app protects your data by switching to "view only" so nothing can be accidentally changed, until the plan is renewed.

## 18. Under the Hood (for the Technical Team)

This section is for developers and system admins; everyday users don't need it.

- **Maintenance mode** — temporarily put the app on a "down for maintenance" page.
- **Offline warning** — a banner appears if the connection drops so nobody mistakes a problem for lost data.
- **Automatic updates** — the app can check for and install the newest version automatically, with version numbering supported.
- **Payment gateway (PipraPay)** — handles online payments such as subscription renewal.
- **Optional technical area** — notes, announcements, and settings reserved for the developer role.

## 19. Connecting Your Online Store

- **Shopify** — connect a Shopify store so orders flow into MamePilot automatically and statuses stay in sync.
- **WooCommerce** — same for WooCommerce stores: orders import automatically and MamePilot reports the status back to your store.
- **PipraPay payments** — collect payments online (for example, invoices or renewals) through the app.
